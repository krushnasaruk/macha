# CaféOS — Official Brand Identity & Logo Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence & Visual Language

CaféOS is an offline-first café management, billing, and operational OS designed for speed, reliability, and warmth. The brand blends the professional precision of modern POS software with the cozy, human craftsmanship of artisanal specialty coffee shops.

The official brand identity centers on our mascot: **Chef Barnaby**, the spectacled bear chef.

## 3. Official Brand Assets

All logo vector files are located in `assets/branding/logo/`:

1. **`cafeos_primary.svg`**: The primary horizontal lockup with mascot icon and wordmark for app headers, invoices, and splash screens.
2. **`cafeos_icon.svg`**: High-precision vector emblem of Chef Barnaby's head with chef hat, spectacle eye patches, and cream muzzle.
3. **`cafeos_wordmark.svg`**: Scalable typography wordmark in Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans geometric sans-serif.
4. **`cafeos_app_icon.png`**: 512×512 launcher icon optimized for Android adaptive icons and iOS squircle masks.
5. **`cafeos_logo_light.svg`**: Inverted lockup for dark surfaces (e.g. tablet POS dark mode, KDS night theme).
6. **`cafeos_logo_dark.svg`**: Dark lockup for clean white receipt thermal printing and bright counter tablets.

## 5. Typography Hierarchy

- **Brand Wordmark**: *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter* Bold (Geometric, legible, friendly curves).
- **Sub-tagline**: Medium tracking (+1.5), uppercase, 60% text opacity.
- **Minimum Digital Sizes**:
  - Primary Lockup: 120px wide × 36px tall.
  - Mascot Icon: 24px × 24px (scalable down to 16px favicon with simplified eyes).
  - Thermal Print Width: 48mm / 80mm monochrome 1-bit raster.

---

## 6. Clear Space & Usage Rules

1. **Clear Space**: Always maintain a clear margin around the logo equal to the height of the letter 'C' in CaféOS.
2. **No Distortion**: Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the mascot head icon.
3. **No Unapproved Colors**: Do not recolor the mascot's tan spectacles or dark fur markings.
4. **Contrast Rule**: On dark backgrounds (`#1E1C1B`), use `cafeos_logo_light.svg` with white typography.
