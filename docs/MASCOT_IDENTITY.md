# CaféOS — Official Mascot Identity & Style Guide

## 1. Character Identity & Concept

- **Character Name**: Chef Barnaby (or "Chef Oso")
- **Role**: Head Chef, Companion & CaféOS Operational Guide
- **Species**: Spectacled Bear (Andean Bear, *Tremarctos ornatus*)
- **Personality**: Calm, dependable, warm, focused, slightly serious default expression with high culinary competence. When busy, he is efficient and reassuring; when a task completes, his celebrations are genuine and earned.
- **Illustration Style**: Flat 2D cartoon, bold clean outlines, solid color fills with subtle ambient occlusion, sticker-like silhouette, zero realistic fur textures, zero 3D glossy effects.

### 2.1 Head and Face
- **Head Silhouette**: Broad, rounded oval head with a subtle wider base. Head width-to-height ratio is approximately 1.15 : 1.
- **Ears**: Two compact, semi-circular ears positioned at the top corners of the head.
- **Fur Color**: Solid charcoal-brown (`#393635` / `RGB(57, 54, 53)`).
- **Eye Markings (Crucial Identity Trait)**: Distinctive tan/light caramel markings (`#D2B18E` / `RGB(210, 177, 142)`) completely encircling both eyes, characteristic of the spectacled bear.
- **Eyes**: Small, solid dark oval pupils (`#171615`), spaced evenly within the tan markings. Expressions are expressed through slight eyelid adjustments, eyebrow angle, or pupil shift—never human eyes with whites or anime glints.
- **Muzzle**: Smooth, rounded cream patch (`#EEE0CF` / `RGB(238, 224, 207)`) extending from between the lower eye patches down to the chin line.
- **Nose**: Prominent horizontal black oval nose (`#171615`) with a vertical philtrum line extending downward.
- **Mouth**: Minimalistic inverted T or subtle curve. Default expression is calm, neutral, and reassuringly professional.

### 2.2 Body & Proportions
- **Proportions**: 2-heads tall overall (1:1 head to body ratio). Chibi/compact silhouette.
- **Body**: Sturdy, pear-shaped rounded torso.
- **Limbs**: Short, rounded arms without articulated fingers (simple rounded paws). Sturdy, wide columnar legs with flat paws for a stable standing stance.

### 2.3 Outfit & Uniform
- **Chef Hat (Toque)**: Small, classic white chef hat with 3–4 soft pleats/puffs, tilted slightly on top of the head between the ears.
- **Apron**: Clean cream-white bib apron (`#F5EDE4`) with straps going over the shoulders and tied behind.
- **Apron Pocket**: Single central curved front pouch with smooth outline (`#9B9289`).

## 4. Strict Identity Preservation Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The uploaded master reference image is the absolute source of truth. Under no circumstance may the character be substituted with another bear or style.

### What MUST Be Preserved:
1. **The Tan Spectacle Markings**: This is the defining feature of the character. Never omit, disconnect, or recolor the tan eye patches.
2. **Solid Charcoal Fur**: Fur must remain dark charcoal-brown. Do not turn the bear into a grizzly brown, polar white, or cartoon yellow.
3. **The Cream Muzzle**: Muzzle must connect under the nose with smooth contours.
4. **Flat 2D Vector/Sticker Aesthetic**: Clean smooth line art, consistent 3–4px line weight at 512px resolution.
5. **Calm Chef Demeanor**: The bear looks like a proud, experienced café head chef. Avoid hyperactive anime tropes or scary expressions.

### What is FORBIDDEN:
- ❌ Do NOT use 3D rendering, claymation textures, or glossy gradients.
- ❌ Do NOT add complex fur strands or realistic animal anatomy.
- ❌ Do NOT remove the chef hat or apron.
- ❌ Do NOT change the eye shape into large human anime eyes.
- ❌ Do NOT add human fingers or claws.
- ❌ Do NOT change outline color or weight randomly between assets.

## 5. Expression & Emotional Spectrum

The mascot expresses emotions through restrained, physical cues:
- **Happy / Pleased**: Small closed-eye arcs `^ ^`, slight upward curve of mouth corner, gentle ear perk.
- **Thinking**: One paw touched to chin/muzzle, eyes tilted upward, slight head tilt.
- **Surprised / Alert**: Slightly widened eye pupils, ears slightly raised, rounded small 'o' mouth.
- **Sleepy / Shift-End**: Half-lidded eyes, chef hat tilted slightly backward, arms resting comfortably on apron.
- **Celebration**: Both paws raised with coffee cup or tray, happy eye arcs, small confetti burst.
- **Concerned / Offline**: Slightly furrowed brow line within tan markings, holding a disconnected plug or coffee mug looking down.
